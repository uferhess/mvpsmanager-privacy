Privacy Policy — M VPS Manager

Last updated: 16 October 2026

Data We Collect

M VPS Manager stores your SSH credentials, panel logins, and server configurations locally on your device. We do not transmit this data to any third-party servers.

Access Control

To verify your access, your Gmail address is checked against an allowlist stored on our server (Cloudflare Workers + D1, at mvps-allowlist.ssnll.com). Only your email address is sent for this check — never your credentials or server data.

Google Sign-In

We use Google Sign-In only to verify your Gmail address for access control. We request the email scope only. We do not access your emails, contacts, or any other Google account data.

Cloudflare

DNS operations use your own Cloudflare API token, sent directly from your device to Cloudflare's servers.

Data Retention & Deletion

All app data lives on your device. Uninstalling the app removes it. To have your Gmail removed from the access allowlist, contact us at the email below.
